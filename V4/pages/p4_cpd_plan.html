The tasks below are a starting point. Change the wording, move the dates, delete what does not fit, add your own.

Why Create usually takes longer than ninety days. Its activities need other people, a review cycle and a published artefact: a community to co-create with, a colleague to coach, a second iteration after feedback. Ninety days is enough to start each of those and rarely enough to finish one. The exceptions that do fit:
  • running this session for your own department — the materials are CC BY, adapting them takes an afternoon;
  • publishing one artefact you already have, with a licence and a commit history;
  • joining an existing practice group rather than founding one.
