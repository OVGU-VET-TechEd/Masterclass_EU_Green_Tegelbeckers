# EDU-204 Research Methods in Education — module information, winter term 2026/27

Fictitious data, written for the llmlab exercise.

## 1 Contact and office hours

Module team: edu204@example.edu. Office hours: Tuesdays 14:00–15:30, room
G40-212, or by video call on request. Emails are answered within three working
days.

## 2 Assessment

The module is assessed by a portfolio (60 %) and a written examination (40 %).

- Portfolio submission deadline: 15 January 2027, 12:00, via the learning
  platform. Each student submits the portfolio personally from their own account.
- Written examination: 9 February 2027, 10:00–12:00, lecture hall H3.
- Resit examination: 30 March 2027, 10:00–12:00, room G40-B05.
- Registration for the written examination is required and closes on
  12 December 2026 in the campus portal.

## 3 Extensions and illness

Extensions to the portfolio deadline are granted only by the examination office
(pruefungsamt@example.edu), not by the module team. The application must be
submitted before the deadline and, in the case of illness, include a medical
certificate. Students who are ill on the day of the examination submit a medical
certificate to the examination office within three working days.

## 4 Grades and review

Results are published in the campus portal by 2 March 2027. Students can inspect
their marked examination in the review session on 5 March 2027, 13:00–15:00,
room G40-212. Requests to change a grade are submitted in writing to the
examination office within one month of publication. The module team does not
discuss grades by email.

## 5 Course materials

Readings are provided on the learning platform in the course "EDU-204 2026/27".
The reading list is in the folder "Week 0".

## 6 Data protection

The module team does not confirm or discuss the enrolment, registration, grades
or circumstances of any student with another person.
